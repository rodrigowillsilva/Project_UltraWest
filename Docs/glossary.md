# Glossary

Shared terms used across design and development.

- Arena: A contained combat space designed for high-mobility encounters.
- Night: A round of combat; survive waves, then receive end-of-night rewards.
- Run: A sequence of nights from start to finish, ending in victory or death.
- Wounded West: The haunted frontier setting of the game.
- Upgrades (Run Upgrades): Temporary power-ups chosen between nights.
- Meta-Progression: Persistent unlocks earned across runs.
- Weapons: Core tools with unique firing and movement abilities.
- Movement Abilities: Mobility tied to specific weapons (e.g., grapple, air burst, focus).
- Focus: Time-slowing state activated via the Crossbow.
- Grapple: Hook-and-pull mobility provided by the Shotgun.
- Air Burst: Directional knock movement from the Grenade Launcher.
- Sprint Buff: Speed increase linked to the Revolver.
- Reload-on-Holster: Weapons reload automatically when not equipped.
